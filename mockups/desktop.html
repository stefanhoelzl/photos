Desktop viewer (DESIGN §11)

The window is maximized with its title bar and closes with ✕. The design is read-only: no settings, upload, set-cover or cache controls. The basemap, tiles and covers are colour stand-ins.

1. Launch: no album selected, so the library's map

Nothing is remembered between launches. The merged DB is rebuilt in memory (~0.5–3 s), and the sidebar shows the phone's loading state meanwhile. The map frames every located album automatically, and an automatic frame narrows nothing: the list is whole, albums without a location included.

2. Moved: the list follows the map

Zoomed onto the Alps and northern Italy. Once the camera settles, the list keeps the albums whose pin is in view, under their containers' headers, counted as a search counts them. Christmas and First steps have no location, so they are gone. The chip's ✕ gives the whole list back and reframes the map. Selecting an album keeps all of this, and Esc (or the sidebar's map icon) comes back to it.

3. A pin clicked: the album on its own map

Rome's pin selects Rome and opens its photos where they were taken, framed afresh. The bar holds only the toggle to the grid, since a map has no tile size. A photo's pin opens the viewer, and a cluster zooms until it splits. The pane keeps the map for the next album that ↑/↓ or a click selects, until the toggle goes back to the grid.

4. Album grid

An album is selected in the sidebar (container headers can't be selected). The main pane shows its photos as square tiles, with the column count following the width.

  • Sidebar: the shared AlbumList at compact size. Only the cover shrinks (54 → 32dp). It keeps the 22dp indents, sticky stacked headers and heavy group-closing lines, and has no cache strip.
  • Sidebar NavBar: sort ⇅, the library's map 🗺 (as Esc) and refresh ⟳ (F5). Large title "Albums" with counts beneath. Search field with the calendar icon (Ctrl+F).
  • Main NavBar: the map toggle, then the S/M/L tile size (also Ctrl+scroll / Ctrl±, 96/160/256dp). Large title = album name, subtitle = count · date span.
  • Keys: arrows move the focus ring (blue outline), Enter opens, ↑/↓ in the sidebar change album, Esc goes back to the library's map.

5. Viewer

Opens in the main pane and the sidebar stays. The main bar collapses to one row. ←/→ page, Esc returns to the grid (scrolled to the photo), Ctrl+scroll or pinch zooms around the cursor, drag pans. Originals are decoded by the native shim, video plays through libvlc.

6. Date filter

The search field's calendar icon opens the shared calendar sheet as an overlay. Once applied, the field shows the range read-only.
