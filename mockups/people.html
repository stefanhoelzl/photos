People in the desktop viewer: the agreed v1

DESIGN §12. Faces are colour stand-ins. photos-cli sync detects the faces and computes suggestions and unknown groups. The viewer reads them from the CLI's cache, writes your verdicts to $LIBRARY_ROOT/.photos/people.db, and the next sync picks them up. The viewer cuts the face crops from the originals itself and caches them on disk, so a tile may show a spinner the first time. Nothing on the phone in v1.

1. A person: reviewing suggestions

A PEOPLE section above the album tree. "Unknown" is a disclosure, collapsed at launch (▸): → or a click on it expands its groups into the sidebar and ← collapses them, with one sidebar and one pane, the same model as albums. ↑/↓ moves through people and albums alike. The right pane is a person: suggestions from the last sync on top, confirmed faces below. Person management in v1 is rename and merge only. There is no delete and no cover choice: the avatar is the most confident confirmed face.

  • Badge = suggestions waiting for review for that person.
  • Selection: arrows move the focus (white ring), and Shift+arrows extend the selection (blue, ✓), as in the album grid. The mouse does the same: click, Shift-click a range, Ctrl-click one.
  • Enter toggles the selection: suggested faces become confirmed, and confirmed faces lose their verdict (back to suggested or unknown at the next sync). N rejects the selection for Anna, and I ignores it. Ctrl+A then Enter confirms everything.
  • Photos ▸ shows the person's photos as the normal photo grid (the phone's People mode would show the same thing).
  • Merge into… moves all of Anna's verdicts to the chosen person and removes Anna. It's how duplicates and wrongly created people are fixed, since there is no delete.
  • Suggestions are for faces whose best match among a person's confirmed faces is above the threshold and clearly ahead of the next person. Rejected pairs are never suggested again. The score is a debugging aid.
  • Confirmations take effect at the next sync: that's when the suggestions improve and the badge updates.
  • Tile spinner: a crop being cut from the original for the first time. After that it comes from the viewer's disk cache.

2. An unknown group: naming it

Cold start. The CLI clustered faces that match nobody. Groups of three or more are listed largest first, and singletons are in "Other". A group opens with every face selected. Space toggles the focused face out (or back in), and Shift+arrows or Ctrl-click adjust ranges. Typing opens the name field (it autocompletes existing people or creates one), and Enter names the selected faces. Deselected faces stay in the group. Delete (or Ignore) gives the selection the ignore verdict — not I, since every letter here belongs to the name (strangers, blurry faces): they're hidden everywhere and never suggested again.

3. The photo viewer with face boxes

F toggles the boxes. Solid white is confirmed, dashed yellow is a suggestion, faint is unknown. Clicking a box opens the same choices as the grids, so any photo can be corrected where you see it. Someone else… opens the name field from the unknown group (autocomplete or a new person). Every choice is a verdict in people.db.
